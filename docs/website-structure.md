# LastStopShop Website Structure

## Purpose

This document defines the intended website/shop structure for LastStopShop before the niche is finalized.

LastStopShop is a Printify + Shopify print-on-demand shop hosted off the Auralis Digital umbrella.

## Placement Under Auralis Digital

```text
Auralis Digital
└── Shop
    ├── Partnered Creators
    ├── Lucid Creations
    ├── RJ Print
    ├── LastStopShop
    └── Etc.
```

## Customer Path

```text
Auralis Digital Shop
        ↓
LastStopShop section / landing page
        ↓
Product or collection page
        ↓
Shopify checkout
        ↓
Printify fulfillment
```

## Main Navigation Recommendation

```text
Home
Shop
```

Inside Shop:

CTA options:

- Shop Products

```text
Pick a product

```

### 6. Trust / Support Section

Include:

## Page Copy Notes

The language should be clean, direct, and not overhyped.

Avoid:

- claiming handmade if Printify fulfills it
- vague marketplace wording
- making it sound like random sellers can join
- promising fast shipping without confirming Printify provider times

Use:

- print-on-demand
- made after purchase
- fulfilled through production partners
- curated products
- Shopify checkout

## Integration Notes

LastStopShop can be integrated into Auralis Digital in one of three ways:

### Option 1 — Linked Shopify Store

Auralis Digital has a LastStopShop page that links customers into Shopify product/collection pages.

Best for early launch.

### Option 2 — Embedded Product Cards

Auralis Digital displays product cards that link to Shopify checkout/product pages.

Best after products are stable.

### Option 3 — Full Shopify-Hosted Shop Section

LastStopShop is operated as its own Shopify storefront or subdomain and linked from Auralis Digital.

Best for long-term scaling.

## Recommended Starting Choice

Start with Option 1: a clean Auralis Digital landing page that sends customers to Shopify product or collection pages.
