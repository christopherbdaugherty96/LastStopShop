# LastStopShop Launch Checklist

## Purpose

This checklist should be completed before LastStopShop is promoted publicly.

## Brand / Structure

- [ ] Confirm niche or temporary broad testing structure.
- [ ] Confirm LastStopShop positioning.
- [ ] Confirm relationship to Auralis Digital.
- [ ] Confirm relationship to Lucid Creations.
- [ ] Confirm relationship to RJ Print.
- [ ] Confirm customer-facing description.

## Shopify

- [ ] Shopify store is created.
- [ ] Store name is correct.
- [ ] Sender email is verified.
- [ ] Contact/support email is visible.
- [ ] Payment provider is active.
- [ ] Payouts are active.
- [ ] Checkout works.
- [ ] Taxes reviewed.
- [ ] Markets/shipping regions reviewed.

## Printify

- [ ] Printify is connected to Shopify.
- [ ] Print providers are selected.
- [ ] Shipping costs reviewed.
- [ ] Production times reviewed.
- [ ] Product variants synced correctly.
- [ ] Product mockups reviewed.
- [ ] Test product created.

## Products

- [ ] Starter products created.
- [ ] Product titles reviewed.
- [ ] Product descriptions reviewed.
- [ ] SEO fields reviewed.
- [ ] Pricing reviewed.
- [ ] Margins reviewed.
- [ ] Images reviewed.
- [ ] Mobile product pages checked.
- [ ] Sizing charts added where needed.

## Policies / Trust

- [ ] Shipping policy added.
- [ ] Return/refund policy added.
- [ ] Privacy policy added.
- [ ] Terms of service added.
- [ ] FAQ added.
- [ ] Order support instructions added.
- [ ] POD/made-after-purchase language is clear.
- [ ] Customer knows who fulfills the product.

## Auralis Digital Integration

- [ ] LastStopShop added under Shop navigation.
- [ ] LastStopShop landing page created or linked.
- [ ] Shopify links work.
- [ ] Product/collection links work.
- [ ] Mobile navigation works.
- [ ] Customer path from Auralis Digital to checkout is clear.

## Final Test

- [ ] Run test checkout.
- [ ] Confirm order confirmation email.
- [ ] Confirm Printify receives order.
- [ ] Confirm fulfillment settings.
- [ ] Confirm support contact.
- [ ] Confirm all public links.
- [ ] Confirm store is ready to promote.
