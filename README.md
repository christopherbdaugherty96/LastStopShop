# LastStopShop

LastStopShop is a dedicated print-on-demand shop identity connected to Shopify through Printify and hosted off the broader Auralis Digital umbrella.

## Current Status

The niche has not been decided yet.

This repo should document and prepare everything needed around the shop structure, brand foundation, Shopify/Printify setup, product planning, customer-facing pages, and launch readiness before the niche is finalized.

## Position Under Auralis Digital

```text
Shop
├── Partnered Creators
├── Lucid Creations
├── RJ Print
├── LastStopShop
└── Etc.
```

## Purpose

LastStopShop is intended to be a separate POD-focused shop/category for future Printify products connected to Shopify while remaining hosted off Auralis Digital.

## Platform Model

```text
Designs / Product Ideas
        ↓
Printify product creation
        ↓
Shopify product listing + checkout
        ↓
Auralis Digital shop navigation / hosted placement
        ↓
Customer purchase
        ↓
Printify fulfillment
```

## Brand Boundaries

- Auralis Digital remains the main umbrella and website hub.
- LastStopShop is its own shop identity under the Shop section.
- LastStopShop is separate from Lucid Creations.
- LastStopShop is separate from RJ Print.
- RJ Print remains focused on 3D printed products.
- Lucid Creations remains focused on Auralis Digital original artwork, design products, and creative POD items.
- LastStopShop can become the broader POD/testing shop once the niche is chosen.

## Known Decisions

- Shop name: LastStopShop
- Business model: print-on-demand
- POD provider: Printify
- Commerce platform: Shopify
- Hosted/linked from: Auralis Digital
- Niche: undecided
- Marketplace model: not random public vendors
- Creator network: trusted friends/family/partnered creators only, if used later
